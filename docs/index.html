Private by design

Nothing leaves your desk.

Hop has no servers at all. Your phone talks straight to your Mac, and everything is encrypted with a key only those two have.

End-to-end encrypted

A fresh AES-256-GCM key for every connection, with protection against replayed or reordered messages.

No cloud, no relay

Your Wi-Fi, your phone's hotspot or a USB cable. Never the internet.

Untrackable signals

The Bluetooth signals change identity every 5 minutes and are signed, so nobody can follow you or fake them.

Respects your privacy settings

Secret notifications stay on the phone, private ones show only their lock-screen version, and the island stays out of screen shares.

No accounts, no analytics

No sign-up, no tracking, no crash reporting. Hop doesn't know who you are.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license and has been security-reviewed. Read it, or build it yourself.

FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Open an issue on GitHub.

How do I install it?

Download both apps. On the Mac, unzip and move Hop.app to Applications, then right-click → Open the first time (it isn't notarized yet). On the phone, open the APK. If Play Protect says it's harmful (it flags any app that reads SMS from outside the Play Store), tap More details → Install anyway, or install over USB with adb install Hop-android.apk. Then choose Pair Phone… in Hop's menu bar and scan the code.

Notification access is greyed out on the phone

Android blocks it for apps installed outside the Play Store. Open Settings → Apps → Hop → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then switch on notification access for Hop.

What do I need for the phone on my Mac?

Install the tools once with brew install scrcpy, turn on USB debugging on the phone and plug it in once. After that it works over Wi-Fi or your phone's hotspot (while Hop on the phone is connected) until the phone restarts.

How does it know the phone is near?

The Mac sends a small signed Bluetooth signal and the phone measures how strong it is, then tells the Mac "near" or "away" over the encrypted link. Fine-tune the distance with Calibrate Distance… in Hop's menu.

Does it drain my battery?

Very little. The phone listens for the Mac in short bursts, and only continuously while the Mac is in range. There's no polling of any server, because there is no server.

Is it really free?

Yes. Hop is open source under the MIT license, with no paid tier, no ads and no accounts.
