**1/4 (with the video)**

I got tired of iPhone users getting all the Mac magic. So I built Hop 🐇

Bring your Android phone near your Mac and it just… appears on screen. Notifications and calls slide out of the notch. One clipboard. Files, codes, all of it.

Free & open source. No cloud, no account. 👇

---

**2/4**

What it does:

📱 Phone pops onto your Mac when it's near, hides when you walk away
🔔 Notifications in the notch. Reply without picking up your phone
📞 Answer calls from your Mac
📋 Copy on one, paste on the other
🔑 Bank codes land straight on your clipboard
🖥 Dock mode: your phone as a full desktop
📁 Drag files onto the phone

---

**3/4**

Privacy was the rule from day one:

• Phone talks to Mac directly, never through a server
• The link is end-to-end encrypted (AES-256)
• No accounts, no analytics, no tracking
• Bluetooth signals are signed and change every 5 minutes, so nobody can follow you

It's all open source, so you can check every line.
